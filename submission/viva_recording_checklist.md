# Viva Recording Checklist

## Before recording

1. Open the slide deck in full-screen presentation mode.
2. Keep the dissertation PDF open for backup reference.
3. Have the application ready on the login or client-selection screen.
4. Check microphone level and screen resolution.
5. Confirm the final talk should stay under 20 minutes.

## During the recording

1. Spend about 12 to 14 minutes on the slides.
2. Spend about 3 to 4 minutes on the live system demo.
3. Name the selected human-evaluation model clearly:
   - `GPT-4.1 Fine-Tuned 2`
4. Say explicitly that the system is:
   - reviewer-facing,
   - not autonomous filing,
   - still subject to human judgement.
5. Mention the real benchmark timings:
   - GPT-5.4 prompt baselines: about `64` to `72` seconds
   - GPT-4.1 Fine-Tuned 2: about `12.4` seconds

## Demo points to show

1. Login page
2. Client-selection page
3. Period / setup page
4. Generated report page
5. Any progress or status updates if available

## Closing line

"The project shows that AI can reduce the effort of producing a first draft for year-end accounting notes, but the output remains most valuable when it is used inside a deterministic, evidence-led, human review workflow."
